Type it. It’s calculated.

Select any of the ten lines and type, or use the keypad. Answers appear immediately. Press Enter or Next to move down a line.

Connect your calculations

On line 3, enter $1 + $2 to add lines 1 and 2. Or choose Ref, then select an earlier line. Only earlier lines can be referenced.

Give reusable numbers a name

Open Variables to edit a separate table of names and numeric values. Use a name directly, such as hourly_rate × 3, or choose Insert beside a variable. Names are case-sensitive. Editing a value updates its calculations and their dependent lines immediately.

See the whole list’s total

The bottom row adds every valid calculation result, including referenced and subtotal lines. Blank, unfinished and invalid lines are skipped; expand the exclusion count to see which ones. Variable-table values are not added directly. The total uses unrounded results and cannot be referenced as another calculation line.

The everyday essentials

Use +, −, ×, ÷ and parentheses. More opens powers, squares, square roots and percentages. For 15% of 200, enter 200 × 15%. A percentage is always divided by 100.

A quiet moment while you type

… means unfinished or waiting on another line. ⓘ means the expression needs attention. Select either for an explanation. Select a result to copy it.

Ready when you come back

Your expressions, variables, selected line and keypad preference save in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes them. Results show integers or two decimals; linked calculations use the unrounded value. Use a decimal point and enter numbers without currency symbols or units.
