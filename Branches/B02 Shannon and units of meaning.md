# B02 Shannon and units of meaning

Optional deviation. Attached to 02 Entropy - a useful wrong turn.

The PDF introduces Shannon as a self-correction, not as a theorem about software intent. Its cited 1948 paper is https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf . This historical reference is carried forward from the supplied PDF; it was not independently rechecked in this revision.

The PDF also proposes a cultural detour through Artemy Lebedev's “unit of meaning” and a comparison with function points. Both can illustrate dissatisfaction with measuring work by lines of code; neither turns our semantic-density metaphor into a shared scientific unit.

Your extension: a compact intent or tool call can have large operational consequences. Much of the behavior comes from pre-existing contracts and implementations, so brevity alone is a poor quality metric.
