# B07 Brooks exact quote and context

Supporting data. Attached to 07 Brooks spoils the ending.

“The hard thing about building software is deciding what one wants to say, not saying it.”

Frederick P. Brooks Jr., No Silver Bullet — Essence and Accidents of Software Engineering. September 1986 technical report TR86-020, section 4.3, “Artificial Intelligence.” The sentence and its location were verified directly in the primary report.[1]

The surrounding discussion concerns AI and expression, including speech recognition; it is not a prediction about today's LLM architecture. Brooks distinguishes conceptual design difficulty from difficulties of representation.[1]

Use the exact quotation unchanged. The modern extrapolation—what happens when expressing an implementation becomes much cheaper—is ours. Do not attribute the extrapolation to Brooks or imply that one quotation proves all present productivity claims.

Stage direction: show the sentence, pause, then reveal “Artificial Intelligence, 1986.” Reprise its idea at the end rather than adding a second long quote.
