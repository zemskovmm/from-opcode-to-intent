# B08 CSharp AST experiment

Optional deviation. Attached to 08 The short prompt has a long context.

Proposed experiment from the discussion: train an autoencoder on structured C# fragments, such as data models or ASTs, and investigate which properties can be recovered from its latent representation.

Define a narrow dataset and a held-out split. Choose candidate properties such as field types, method structure, or nesting before probing. Compare against a simple baseline so a successful probe is not automatically credited to an interesting learned abstraction.

Reconstruction quality, decodability of a property, and a causal editable “semantic axis” are different findings. Do not claim the strongest one from evidence for the weakest.

No dataset, training run, or results have been supplied or produced for this talk. This is a potential follow-up project.

Best placement: Q&A or another talk, not an additional timed trunk section. If mentioned on stage, one sentence inside shared context is enough.
